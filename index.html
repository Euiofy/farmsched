ABOUT THIS PROJECT

About Teyvat Companion

A free, unofficial companion for planning your next adventure in Genshin Impact.

Made for Travelers

Teyvat Companion helps you decide what to farm, organize character and weapon upgrades, and keep track of your progress without needing to sign into your game account.

Your adventure tools

Paimon's Journey Book — Plan character and weapon farming around domain rotations and your selected server.
Material Schedule — Browse daily materials and see which characters or weapons need them.
Inventory Board — Track saved materials and targets.
Build Planner — Organize your roster, talent goals, and teams.

Developer

Created by Euiofy as an independent personal project.

EuiofyGitHub profile↗

Privacy and reminders

Plans, inventory, and preferences are stored on your device. Export a backup in Settings before changing devices or browsers. Browser notifications require permission; optional phone reminders use ntfy. No game credentials are required.

Credits

Game data and artwork are based on Genshin Impact and public resources including Enka.Network, Yatta, Genshin Builds, Paimon.moe and community wikis. All game assets belong to their respective owners.

Unofficial fan project

Not affiliated with or endorsed by HoYoverse. Verify domain rewards in-game after updates.
